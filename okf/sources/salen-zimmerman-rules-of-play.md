**Claim this bundle leans on.** **Meaningful play** has two conditions. An outcome is
**discernable** when the player can perceive that an action had a result, and
**integrated** when that result matters later in the game. A game is a system, and the
designer designs the *rules* that produce play, never the play itself (second-order
design).

**What it settles here.** The author's third belief, *"a game provides a space for the
player to receive feedback from the world,"* is the discernable condition; the belief that
upgrades carry forward is the integrated one. Both are properties of the **world's
response to the avatar**, so [what a game is](/concepts/what-a-game-is.md) §3 makes
feedback a first-class part of the avatar concept rather than a presentation detail.
Second-order design is also the forge/artifact line restated: the forge authors rules,
the artifact produces play.
