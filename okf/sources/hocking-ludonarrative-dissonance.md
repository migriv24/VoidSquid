**Claim this bundle leans on.** BioShock's *mechanics* reward self-interest (harvesting
Little Sisters pays) while its *narrative* condemns Objectivism; the two contracts the
game offers the player are in conflict, and the conflict is felt as dissonance.

**What it settles here.** If the upgrade graph is where the game's values are written
([Bogost](/sources/bogost-procedural-rhetoric.md)) and the narrative is a second place
where they are written, the forge holds **two statements of the same thesis** and can
compare them. That is a structural check, not a taste call: *does any `unlocks` edge
reward what a `beat` condemns?* It is the kind of query a Void Core mantle can answer
and a conventional engine cannot, because the engine does not hold the narrative as
structure. Recorded in [what a game is](/concepts/what-a-game-is.md) §4 as a claim to
test, not a feature to build.
