**Claim this bundle leans on.** Play is *voluntary*, *outside ordinary life*, *absorbing*,
*bounded in time and space*, and *ordered by rules*; inside the bounded space the ordinary
world's laws are replaced by the game's. Later writers call that bounded space the
**magic circle**, from a phrase Huizinga uses in passing while listing play-grounds (the
arena, the card table, the temple, the stage).

**What it settles here.** The author's belief that a game is a *world* the player enters,
and that the world's rules are the game's own, is Huizinga's. In Void Core's terms the
magic circle is a **mantle**: a bounded set of runes and rules with the outside reachable
only through holidays. [What a game is](/concepts/what-a-game-is.md) §2 takes the world
as the first noun for that reason.
