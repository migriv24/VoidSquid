**Claim this bundle leans on.** Play comes in four kinds: **agon** (competition),
**alea** (chance), **mimicry** (simulation, role-taking) and **ilinx** (vertigo), each
running from **paidia** (spontaneous, improvised) to **ludus** (rule-bound, structured).

**What it settles here.** The author's line between *our* games and Tetris or Candy
Crush is a Caillois line. Tetris is agon-ludus with no mimicry: rules without a world or a
role. The games Void Squid exists to make are heavy in **mimicry** (an avatar, a role, a
narrative) and move along the paidia–ludus axis as the player goes from exploring a world
to mastering its rules. Dungeons & Dragons is the clearest case of all four at once under a
referee. See [what a game is](/concepts/what-a-game-is.md) §6.
